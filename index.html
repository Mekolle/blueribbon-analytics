Sound familiar?

Getting the full picture of your business means checking different systems and piecing the numbers together, from JobTread and QuickBooks to your CRM, spreadsheets and marketing agency reports.

01

You need facts to make decisions, but you’re still relying on gut feeling.

Can you afford another hire? Which jobs are making money? Should you increase your marketing budget? You need clear numbers to answer these questions, but getting them isn’t straightforward.

02

You’re spending time finding numbers when you could be selling.

Checking job costs, pulling sales figures and updating spreadsheets takes time. You want to spend that time meeting potential clients, following up on estimates and winning your next project.

03

You’re paying people to deliver, but you can’t clearly measure their performance.

Are your salespeople turning appointments into signed contracts? Are your contractors keeping projects on schedule and within budget? Is your marketing agency bringing in leads that become paying clients? You need to see where things are working and where to step in.

04

You need numbers you can trust without checking everything yourself.

A job looks profitable, but some costs haven’t come through. Two reports show different totals. You find yourself going back to the source to check what’s right before making a decision.

Questions

Questions Builders ask first.

Still deciding? Here's what business owners usually ask before we get started.

01Is Blueribbon a good fit for my business?

Blueribbon is built for small businesses where leadership is close to operations and important information lives across multiple systems. We often work directly with owners, but a director or department leader can also lead the project when the goal is to give management a clearer view of the business. Industry, headcount and company age matter less than whether the current reporting setup is creating a real visibility problem.

02How long does a reporting system take to build?

The core offer is to get your reporting system live in 30 days. During the Understand phase, if the scope or source systems require more time, we will identify that early and agree the timeline before the full build proceeds.

03How do you price a project?

Projects start at $2,500. The final price depends on the number and complexity of data sources, integration requirements and reporting scope. You receive a clear proposal once we understand what the system needs to do.

04Do I have to pay the full project cost upfront?

No. Projects use milestone-based payments: 40% for Understand + Connect, 40% after the core system is built and demonstrated, and 20% at Launch.

05What happens after the system goes live?

Ongoing management can keep the system running, accurate, relevant and improving as your business changes. Larger expansions, such as new data sources or major new reporting areas, are scoped separately.

06Do you only work in Power BI?

Power BI is the main reporting interface I use, but the client is buying a reporting system, not a dashboard tool. The work can include source connections, APIs, data transformation, modeling and, where the project requires it, a data warehouse.

07Who owns the reporting system?

You do. The reporting system is built in your environment wherever practical, and your business retains ownership and control of the system and its data. Blueribbon is given the access needed to build and, if you choose ongoing Reporting System Management, continue working on it.

08How do you handle data security?

Access is limited to what is required to build and manage the reporting system. Client data and reporting environments remain under the client's control, and NDAs can be signed where required. Specific architecture and access arrangements are agreed for each project.
