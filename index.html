The process

Questions

The answers to your questions

Still deciding? Here's what business owners usually ask before we get started.

01
Why should I work with Blueribbon Analytics?

Because you get me directly, not a rotating cast of juniors. With a finance background and 11+ years of experience, I build reporting systems around the decisions your business actually makes, then stay on to keep it sharp as things change.

02
What if my business doesn't fit one of the industries you listed?

Those are just where I've built the most reporting systems so far. If your business runs on scattered spreadsheets and manual reports, reach out anyway, I'll tell you honestly whether it's a good fit.

03
How long does a build take?

Most reporting systems go from discovery call to live dashboard in 2 to 3 weeks, depending on how many data sources need connecting and how much cleanup they need.

04
Do you offer support after the dashboard is built?

Yes. Every build includes training so your team can read and adjust it themselves, plus ongoing support as your business and questions evolve.

05
How do you price a project?

Pricing depends on how many data sources are involved and how complex your reporting needs are. The discovery call is where we scope that out together, so you get a clear number before any work starts.

06
How do you handle data security?

Your data stays inside your own data warehouse and Power BI environment. I don't copy it into third party tools, and access is limited to what's needed to build and maintain your reporting system. I also sign NDAs and never share your work, data, or dashboards without your permission.
