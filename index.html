The process

Understand. Connect. Build. Launch.

A clear 30-day implementation process built around the decisions you need to make, not a generic dashboard template.

01

Understand

We map the decisions you make, the questions you need answered, the reports you use today, and where the required data lives. This becomes the blueprint for the reporting system.

02

Connect

We establish automated access to the required source data. The reporting system pulls the data from the source automatically, so your team is not rebuilding the same reports by hand.

03

Build

We build the reporting system around three questions: What is happening? Where should I look? Why is it happening? Every view has a job to do.

04

Launch

We validate the numbers, deploy the system, train your team, and put it into day-to-day use. Every implementation includes 30 days of Reporting System Management after launch.

A cardiac clinic in Panama needed a reliable way to see outstanding invoices without repeatedly logging into its accounting software and piecing the answer together. I connected the reporting directly to the accounting source and built a live view of outstanding balances and aging. 02 Cleaning & restoration · Weekly performance

From a Monday export to an always-current management view

Every Monday, someone exported data to Excel to prepare incomplete performance numbers, with little context on whether results were improving or declining. The source system had no API, so I built an automated email-based pipeline, added commission logic and data checks, and turned the weekly reporting task into an always-current management view that shows what changed, where to look, and why.

I built an RFM model around recency, frequency and value so management could see what was happening across the customer base, identify the segments that deserved attention, and understand why customers were classified as loyal, promising or at risk.

Questions

The answers to your questions

Still deciding? Here's what business owners usually ask before we get started.

01
Is Blueribbon a good fit for my business?

Blueribbon is built for small businesses where leadership is close to operations and important information lives across multiple systems. We often work directly with owners, but a director or department leader can also lead the project when the goal is to give management a clearer view of the business. Industry, headcount and company age matter less than whether the current reporting setup is creating a real visibility problem.

02
How long does a reporting system take to build?

The core offer is to get your reporting system live in 30 days. During the Understand phase, if the scope or source systems require more time, we will identify that early and agree the timeline before the full build proceeds.

03
How do you price a project?

Projects start at $2,500. The final price depends on the number and complexity of data sources, integration requirements and reporting scope. You receive a clear proposal once we understand what the system needs to do.

04
Do I have to pay the full project cost upfront?

No. Projects use milestone-based payments: 40% for Understand + Connect, 40% after the core system is built and demonstrated, and 20% at Launch.

05
What happens after the system goes live?

Every implementation includes 30 days of Reporting System Management. After that, ongoing management can keep the system running, accurate, relevant and improving as your business changes. Larger expansions, such as new data sources or major new reporting areas, are scoped separately.

06
Do you only work in Power BI?

Power BI is the main reporting interface I use, but the client is buying a reporting system, not a dashboard tool. The work can include source connections, APIs, data transformation, modeling and, where the project requires it, a data warehouse.

07
Who owns the reporting system?

You do. The reporting system is built in your environment wherever practical, and your business retains ownership and control of the system and its data. Blueribbon is given the access needed to build and, if you choose ongoing Reporting System Management, continue working on it.

08
How do you handle data security?

Access is limited to what is required to build and manage the reporting system. Client data and reporting environments remain under the client's control, and NDAs can be signed where required. Specific architecture and access arrangements are agreed for each project.
