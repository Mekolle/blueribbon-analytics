The Blueribbon Reporting Framework

Built around the questions you actually need answered

A useful reporting system should do more than show charts. It should help management move from the big picture to the issue that needs attention, then into the reason behind it.

01 · What’s happening?

See the overall health of the business

Revenue, jobs, pipeline, collections, marketing and operational KPIs give you the management picture first.

02 · Where should I look?

Know what needs your attention

Spot weak service lines, aging invoices, unusual job performance, underperforming lead sources or other exceptions.

03 · Why is it happening?

Drill into the driver behind the number

Break performance down by job, service line, crew, customer, referral source, campaign, location or period where your data supports it.

Questions before you book

01
How long does it take?

The standard implementation target is 30 days. If your systems or requirements genuinely need longer, I will tell you during the Understand phase before the full build proceeds.

02
Which cleaning or restoration software can you connect to?

Most job management, field-service, accounting, CRM and marketing platforms that provide a usable connection or export. Tell me what you use and I will confirm the practical connection options before you commit.

03
Have you worked with restoration or cleaning companies before?

Yes. The reporting system at the top of this page was built for CleanUSA, a cleaning and restoration company, and I have delivered 450+ across many industries. I can walk you through it on the call.

04
Do I need to buy anything extra?

Depending on your setup, you may need Microsoft licenses or low-cost cloud infrastructure. Not every project needs a data warehouse. Any expected third-party costs are identified before the build.

05
What does it cost?

Projects start at $2,500. Final pricing depends on the systems being connected, data complexity and reporting scope. Payments are milestone-based: 40% to start, 40% after the Build milestone and 20% at Launch.

06
Who owns the reporting system and the data?

You do. Your business retains ownership and control of the reporting system and its data. Blueribbon is given the access needed to build it and, if you choose ongoing Reporting System Management, continue working on it.

07
What happens after it goes live?

Every implementation includes the first 30 days of Reporting System Management. That covers keeping the existing system running, accurate, relevant and improving through reasonable refinements. Ongoing management is optional and is priced upfront in the proposal.
